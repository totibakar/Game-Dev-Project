# The Forgotten Past : Introduction

The Forgotten Past is a horror game set in the 90s where the player is a horror vlogger who explores an abandoned school in the middle of nowhere. He aims to uncover the truth of a forgotten past where people come but never return and are forgotten by the world. Through obstacles & clues, our protagonist will soon discover that they are not alone in the premise and it is coming to hunt the intruder.
